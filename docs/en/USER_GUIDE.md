# User Guide — Dumbhanengue ERP

A simple guide to using your everyday business system: stock, sales, customers and money, all in one place.

---

## 1. Signing in

1. Open the **Dumbhanengue** app on your phone.
2. Enter your **email** and **password**.
3. Tap **Sign in**.

> **First time?** Tap **"I don't have an account — sign up"**, fill in your details and wait for your access to be approved (up to 24h). Once approved, you can sign in normally.

---

## 2. Home screen (Home)

The home screen shows your business at a glance:

- **Total sales** for today
- **Cash balance**
- **Receivables (credit)**
- **Stock value**
- **Alerts** ⚠️ (see below)

### Important alerts
- 🟡 **Yellow** = product **expiring soon** (less than 30 days left).
- 🔴 **Red** = product **already expired** but still in stock.
- Tap the alert to see which product and batch it involves.

### Quick actions
The main buttons are on the home screen: **New sale**, **Add product**, **Register transaction** and **Register credit**.

---

## 3. Products

### Adding a product
1. Open **Products** → tap **New product**.
2. Fill in: **name**, **purchase price** and **sale price**.
3. **Stock quantity**: enter how many units you have (e.g. `50`).
4. **Expiry (optional)**: tap **Set expiry date** and pick the expiration date. The system will warn you when it's about to expire.
5. **Sell by weight?** Turn on **"Sell by weight (fraction)"** if the product is sold by kilo/litre (e.g. bulk rice, bulk oil) and choose the unit (kg, g, l, ml). This lets the checkout accept quantities like `1.75 kg`.
6. Tap **Add product**.

> 💡 The **barcode (PLU)** is used by label-printing scales. If your scale uses the `20PPPPPWWWWW` format, the system reads the label and adds the correct weight to the sale automatically.

---

## 4. Sales (Checkout)

1. Tap **New sale**.
2. Search for the product or **scan the barcode** (camera reader or scale).
3. Adjust the quantity (weight products accept decimals: `1.75`).
4. Choose the payment method (cash, M-Pesa, E-Mola, card...).
5. Tap **Confirm sale**.

> ✅ Stock is deducted automatically — oldest batch first (FIFO), so nothing sits and expires.

---

## 5. Purchases (stock intake)

1. Tap **New purchase**.
2. Enter the **supplier**.
3. Add the purchased products and quantities.
4. **Expiry (optional)**: for products that expire, tap the calendar icon and pick the batch date.
5. Confirm the purchase. Stock increases immediately.

---

## 6. Customers and Credit

- **Customers**: register your customers with name and phone.
- **Register credit**: when a customer takes goods to pay later, register the amount and due date.
- The **Credit** screen shows:
  - **Pending** = who still owes (total receivable).
  - **Settled** = payment history.
- When the customer pays, tap **Settle debt** — the money enters the cash register automatically.

---

## 7. Finances

- **Register transaction**: money in (income) and money out (expenses).
- **Pending (credit)**: tick this if the amount hasn't been received yet — it only counts as cash once received.
- The screen shows **income**, **expenses** and the **balance**.

---

## 8. Reports

See how your business is doing:

- **Revenue** (how much you sold in the period)
- **Gross profit** and **margin**
- **Best sellers** (top products)
- **Sales per day**

Use the filters: **Today**, **7 days**, **This month**, **All**.

---

## 9. Stores and Employees (owner/manager)

- **Stores**: register your stores (e.g. Central Store, Neighbourhood Store). Each store has its own stock and cash register.
- **Employees**: create accounts for the people who work with you and choose the role:
  - **Manager**: operates everything except store management.
  - **Employee**: sells only (cannot add products or view reports).

> 💡 Switch the active store with the selector at the top of the screen — everything (stock, sales, cash) follows the chosen store.

---

## 10. Quick tips

- 🧾 Always double-check the **total** before confirming a sale.
- ⚠️ Check the **expiry alerts** every week — expired product is wasted money.
- 📱 In every list (sales, products, customers...) **pull down** to refresh.
- 🔒 The app stores your session securely — you only need to sign in once.
